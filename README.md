## The model to research

Find suitable local businesses, reach an owner/decision-maker, offer a free ranking demonstration, show a measured improvement after roughly one week, then ask whether they want to pay monthly for continuing service.

The completed study **assumes the provider delivers its stated ranking improvements**, as instructed. **Preferred prospects are eligible shops currently around positions 4–6 on high-value searches**, not shops already comfortably in the Top 3. The founder's hours are **not valued as an hourly CAC or expense**; track out-of-pocket cash CAC and use time only for workload planning. Retention is a primary ongoing responsibility, supported by understandable call, booking and revenue reports. Actual retention has not yet been measured.

The provider has offered to rank **three businesses free**. Working economics are $300/location/month wholesale; the completed report recommends testing **$697 standard** and **$597 founding**, with $500 a thinner-margin comparison. These prices are not yet validated by actual sales.

The week-long demonstration concerns rankings. Completed jobs/revenue within a week are not assumed.
