## The model to research

Find suitable local businesses, reach an owner/decision-maker, offer a free ranking demonstration, show a measured improvement after roughly one week, then ask whether they want to pay monthly for continuing service.

For this study, **assume the provider delivers its stated ranking improvements**, as Mitch instructed. Research the opportunity to build a profitable agency around that capability: demand, competition, customer selection, acquisition, sales, pricing, operational effort and retention. Do not make provider access or another effectiveness investigation a prerequisite.

The provider has offered to rank **three businesses free**. Working economics are $300/location/month wholesale and $500 retail before other expenses; $597/$697 alternatives and add-ons remain ideas.

The week-long demonstration concerns rankings. Completed jobs/revenue within a week are not assumed.
