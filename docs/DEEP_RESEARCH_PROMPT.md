Important context:
- I have NOT joined the provider and do NOT currently have its grid tool. Do not assume access or make getting it a prerequisite for this research.
- The provider is Jumper Local/Jumper Media. Read the attached Agency Reseller Tool Kit PDF if available; otherwise use the detailed, page-referenced repo summary.

Research broadly across actual agency offers, original data, experienced operators, buyer discussions, case studies and failed approaches. Investigate:

6. Pricing, CAC, conversion scenarios, retention, founder workload, break-even and profit at 5/10/20/50 clients.
7. Ongoing value and reasons clients stay or cancel after an initial ranking win.

Give me a candid recommendation, concrete competitor and vertical comparisons, realistic economics, and a 30/60/90-day launch plan including the first three trials. Explain material risks briefly; do not turn the study into another investigation of whether the provider works.

Cite sources, verify current prices/offers, distinguish evidence from anecdotes and assumptions, and do not invent results or benchmarks.
