## What this dataset cannot demonstrate

- A company's actual rank citywide or on a particular local search point: requires geo-grid scans.
- That the company has an underperforming share of impressions/clicks/calls: requires Google Business Profile performance access or other carefully interpreted estimates.
- That a vendor drive caused a search-ranking change: requires a measured pilot and controlled interpretation.
- A prospect's current owner, business revenue/margins, willingness to pay, or acquisition cost: needs sales qualification.
- That the raw source order is comparable across the three queries: it is **not**.
- That places with strong review counts will definitely win customers: commercial performance is unknown.
