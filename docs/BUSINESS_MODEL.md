### Upsell illustrative math

At 20 base clients, $500 pricing and $300 vendor cost: $4,000 monthly base contribution. If 5 of these 20 buy a $150/month add-on, that adds $750 **revenue**, not necessarily $750 profit; subtract production, writing, publishing, account management and external costs. A prior illustrative calculator treated add-ons as zero direct cost only to model upside; real costs must be tracked.

## Unit economics for qualification

A $500 monthly service should be tied to client job contribution and probability of acquiring new customers, not just raw average map rank. Example: If an incremental closed repair job contributes $800, one added job every ~1.6 months would cover $500/month *on average*; lead-to-sale conversion and attribution need actual measurement. For high-ticket roofing/collision jobs, fewer leads may cover spend, but job margin varies widely.

To consider a prospect eligible, collect or conservatively estimate:

- Average sale, gross profit/job or contribution margin.
- Lead-to-customer close rate and time lag.
- Target map keywords and actual volume/intent, where supported.
- Present map visibility and competitor review parity.
- Capacity to handle new leads and expected geographic service area.

## Acquisition model / unknowns

**Current reporting rule:** track actual **cash** acquisition spending and divide by paid clients acquired. Do not assign a hypothetical dollar cost to founder sales or trial hours. Hours may be logged only to assess capacity and later delegation.

A previous discussion used this **illustrative—not observed—monthly funnel**:

| Funnel step | Planning-only assumption |
|---|---:|
| Qualified prospects contacted | 200 |
| Positive response rate | 5% = 10 |
| Conversations from responses | 60% = 6 |
| Trial agreement rate | 50% = 3 |
| Paid conversion from trials | 50% = ~1–2 |

Track actual contacted, valid decision-maker contacts, responses, meetings, trials, paid conversions, days-to-close, average acquisition effort and cancellation. These planning benchmarks should be revised after the first 100–200 prospects.

### Contribution LTV sensitivity (excludes time value and support costs)

At $200 gross monthly contribution: 3-month retention = $600 contribution; 6 months = $1,200; 12 months = $2,400. Actual CAC must fit within realized contribution after fulfillment labor/support and churn. Avoid assuming every new client renews for a full year.

## White-label vendor questions / contractual due diligence

1. Identity, operating history, method details, relationship with game participants, truthful service description and risk allocation.
2. **Three** offered free business trials: exact eligibility, limits, term, whether card required, activation fees, what is included, whether billing automatically begins.
3. What data demonstrates sustained results rather than isolated week-long lifts? Original keyword, grid resolution, geo center, dates, competitor context and engagement outcomes.
4. Vendor service area and hidden-address limitations; minimum address, reviews, listing verification requirements.
5. Suspension/negative outcomes, warranty, refunds, cancellations, and notice periods.
6. Exclusion/exclusivity if selling to direct competitors in the same micro-market.
7. Reporting ownership, export rights, white-label branding, and client data rights.
8. Volume rate breaks: $300 down to $250 or $200 as clients scale.
9. Whether routine GBP/on-site SEO by our agency affects or conflicts with their method.
10. Terms/policy compliance, future ranking algorithm volatility, and client disclosure obligations.

## Risk register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Dependence on one ranking vendor | High | Build independent prospecting, audit, GBP and on-site SEO offerings; pilot more than one fulfillment type |
| Policy/algorithm disruption of artificial engagement | High | No ranking guarantees; disclose material risk; maintain compliant alternate services and clear cancellation |
| Ranking rises but clients get few calls | High | Review parity, high-intent keyword selection, monitor GBP actions/calls/CRM outcome |
| Outreach skepticism / poor decision-maker reach | High | Useful baseline report, targeted personal outreach, case studies and low-risk offer |
| $200 contribution insufficient after support | Medium-high | Track CAC and labor, test $597/$697, standardize workflows, negotiate vendor volume discounts |
| Prospect research data is misleading | Medium-high | Deduplicate by Place ID; distinguish Outscraper order from grid; repeat matched scans |
| Multiple competing clients in one location | Medium | Define customer category/geographic policy, avoid contradictory guaranteed positions |

## Success gates

**Pilot phase:** get vendor terms in writing; baseline independent grid scans; qualify 5 businesses and run 2–3 representative trials; measure matched pre/post and actual actions if accessible; test outreach to >=100 qualified prospects.

**Scale phase:** establish repeatable positive paid conversion and financially sustainable customer retention; document revenue contribution and workflow cost; only then invest meaningfully in a custom Base44 prospecting product.
